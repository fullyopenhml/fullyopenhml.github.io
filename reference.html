Naming

  • Part IDs: 3DP_ printed, ACT_ actuator, HW_ bearings and metal, EL_ electronics, CB_ cables, MAT_ filament, TL_ tools. Printed IDs continue with a subassembly code (pel, leg, tor, hea, arm, grp) and a two-digit number, then a descriptive name.
  • Occurrences add the side: knee_L_act, shank_inner_R. Mirrored prints share one part ID.
  • Joints: hip_pitch_L, knee_R, waist; CAN ID = 10 × limb + index (left leg 11–16, right leg 21–26, left arm 31–35, right arm 41–45, waist 1).
  • Links are named by the part that carries them: hip_roll_bracket_L, thigh_L, shank_L, ankle_block_L, foot_L.
