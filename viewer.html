About this model

Every part is built from simple solids placed by tools/build_data.py; the printable STLs on the printing page are the same solids with the hole patterns, pockets and recesses cut. Actuator envelopes and interfaces follow the RobStride manuals. Fillets and cable channels are not modelled.
