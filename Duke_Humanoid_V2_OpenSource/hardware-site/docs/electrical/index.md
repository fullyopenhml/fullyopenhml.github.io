# Electrical

Two packs in series feed a 48 V bus to 31 actuators through four distribution blocks; six CAN buses carry the commands; 12 V feeds the computer and the gripper servos; USB carries cameras, IMU and grippers. Make every cable from the tables below, then run the checks before a pack is connected.
